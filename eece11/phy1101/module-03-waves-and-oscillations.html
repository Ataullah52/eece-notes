Differential Equation of Damped Oscillation & Quality Factor (Q)

P1 · Class Note · Definition & Law · Class Note · Lecture 02 (Mechanical-Electrical Analogy)

\(\frac{d^{2}x}{dt^{2}} + 2b(\frac{dx}{dt}) + \omega _{0}^{2}x = 0\) where \(b = \frac{r}{2m}\) is the damping coefficient and \(\omega _{0} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\) is the natural angular frequency.

A real oscillating system experiences a resistive damping force proportional to velocity \((F_{d} = -\frac{r\cdot dx}{dt})\) in addition to the linear restoring force (\(F_{r} = -\)kx). This second-order linear ODE mirrors a series RLC electrical circuit where mass m ↔ inductance \(L\), damping r ↔ resistance \(R\), and compliance \(\frac{1}{k} ↔\) capacitance \(C\).
