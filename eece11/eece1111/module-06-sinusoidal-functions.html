Phasor Representation, Complex Impedance (Z) & Admittance (Y) of RL, RC and RLC Branches

P1 · Class Note · Core Theory & Concept · Class Note · Sinusoidal Steady-State, Phasors & RLC Branch Impedance

Impedance \(Z = R + jX = |Z|\angle \theta (\Omega )\) and Admittance \(Y = \frac{1}{Z} = G + jB (S)\), where \(X_{L} = \omega L\) and \(X_{C} = \frac{1}{\omega C}\).

A sinusoidal waveform \(v(t) = V_{m} \cos(\omega t + \varphi )\) is represented in the phasor domain as \(V = V_{m}\angle \varphi \) (or in RMS form as \((\frac{V_{m}}{\sqrt{2}})\angle \varphi \)). Time differentiation \(\frac{d}{dt}\) corresponds to multiplying by \(j\omega \), and integration \(\int dt\) corresponds to dividing by \(j\omega \).

In an inductive branch (RL), voltage leads current by phase angle \(\theta = \tan^{-1}(\frac{X_{L}}{R}) > 0\). In a capacitive branch (RC), current leads voltage \((\theta = -\tan^{-1}(\frac{X_{C}}{R}) < 0)\).

Table 6.1 — Impedance, Reactance, Admittance & Susceptance of Passive Elements
Element / BranchImpedance \(Z = R + jX (\Omega )\)Admittance \(Y = G + jB (S)\)Phase Angle Relation
Resistor (R)\(Z_{R} = R + j0\)\(Y_{R} = G = \frac{1}{R}\)\(v(t)\) and \(i(t)\) in phase \((\theta = 0^{\circ})\)
Inductor (L)\(Z_{L} = j\omega L = jX_{L}\)\(Y_{L} = -\frac{j}{\omega L} = -jB_{L}\)\(v(t)\) leads \(i(t)\) by \(90^{\circ}\)
Capacitor (C)\(Z_{C} = -\frac{j}{\omega C} = -jX_{C}\)\(Y_{C} = j\omega C = +jB_{C}\)\(i(t)\) leads \(v(t)\) by \(90^{\circ}\)
Series RLC Branch\(Z = R + j(X_{L} - X_{C})\)\(Y = \frac{R - j(X_{L} - X_{C})}{R^{2} + (X_{L} - X_{C})^{2}}\)\(\tan \theta = \frac{X_{L} - X_{C}}{R}\)

Series RLC Branch Impedance, Admittance, Power Factor & Average Power

P2 · Previous Year Question · Previous Year Question · Previous Year Question · PUST EECE Final Exam 2023 Q6(a) & 2021 Q6(b) · Asked in: 2023, 2021 (7 Marks)

Calculate Z, Y, current phasor \(I\), power factor \(\cos \theta \), and average power \(P = V_{rms} I_{rms} \cos \theta \) for a series RLC branch.

Problem / Theorem Question

A series RLC branch with \(R = 8 \Omega \), inductive reactance \(X_{L} = 12 \Omega \), and capacitive reactance \(X_{C} = 6 \Omega \) is connected across an AC voltage source \(v(t) = 100\sqrt{2} \cos(314t) V\). Find (1) total impedance \(Z\) and admittance \(Y\)

(2) RMS current \(I_{rms}\) and phase angle \(\theta \)

(3) power factor and average power absorbed.

Step-by-step solution
Step 1: Compute \(Z, Y, I_{rms}\), and Average Power \(P\)

Combine net reactance \(X = X_{L} - X_{C} = 12 - 6 = 6 \Omega \):

\(Z = 8 + j(12 - 6)\)

\(= 8 + j6\)

\(= 10\angle 36.87^{\circ} \Omega \)

\(Y = \frac{1}{Z}\)

\(= \frac{8 - j6}{100}\)

\(= 0.08 - j0.06 S\)

\(I_{rms} = \frac{V_{rms}}{Z}\)

\(= \frac{100\angle 0^{\circ}}{10\angle 36.87^{\circ}}\)

\(= 10\angle -36.87^{\circ} A\)

Power factor: pf \(= \cos(36.87^{\circ})\)

\(= 0.8\) (lagging)

Average power: \(P = V_{rms} I_{rms} \cos \theta \)

\(= 100 \times 10 \times 0.8\)

∴ \(Z = 10\angle 36.87^{\circ} \Omega , I_{rms} = 10\angle -36.87^{\circ} A, P = 800 W\) (pf \(= 0.8\) lagging)

Instantaneous Power \(p(t)\), Average Power \(P\), Reactive Power Q & Complex Power \(S\)

P3 · Professor-Followed Book · Core Theory & Concept · Professor-Followed Book · B. L. Theraja Vol. I (Ch. 11–13) & Alexander & Sadiku (Ch. 9)

Complex power \(S = V_{rms} I_{rms}* = P + jQ (VA)\), where \(P = V_{rms} I_{rms} \cos \theta (W)\) and \(Q = V_{rms} I_{rms} \sin \theta \) (VAR).

Complex, Active & Reactive Power Triangle
\(S = P + jQ = V_{rms} I_{rms}^* | |S| = \sqrt{P^{2} + Q^{2}}\), pf \(= \cos \theta = P / |S|\)

Series-Parallel Admittance Conversion (Conductance G & Susceptance \(B\))

P4 · Syllabus Book · Worked Numerical Example · Syllabus Book · Corcoran & Kerchner & Robert L. Boylestad (Ch. 15)

For a parallel RL or RC branch, sum the conductances \(G = \sum G_{k}\) and susceptances \(B = \sum B_{k}\) directly.

Series Impedance \((R + jX)\) to Parallel Admittance \((G + jB)\)
\(G = \frac{R}{R^{2} + X^{2}}, B = -\frac{X}{R^{2} + X^{2}}\)
